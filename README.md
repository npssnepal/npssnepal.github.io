**Nepal Physical Students’ Society (NPSS)** is a student-led academic and scientific community dedicated to bringing together students of physics and related disciplines across Nepal. NPSS aims to promote interest in physics, scientific research, innovation, academic collaboration, and professional development among students.

The society provides a platform for students to **learn, connect, collaborate, and contribute to science** through seminars, workshops, conferences, exhibitions, research activities, science communication, competitions, and student-led academic programs.

## Our Vision

To build a strong, collaborative, and inclusive community of physics students that contributes to the advancement of **physics, scientific research, education, and innovation in Nepal**.

## Our Mission

* Promote physics education and scientific curiosity among students.
* Encourage undergraduate and graduate students to participate in research.
* Create opportunities for interaction between students, researchers, academics, and professionals.
* Organize seminars, workshops, conferences, exhibitions, and scientific outreach programs.
* Support students in developing academic, research, communication, and leadership skills.
* Encourage collaboration among physics students and institutions across Nepal.
* Connect Nepali physics students with national and international scientific communities.
* Promote the participation of students in emerging areas of physics, technology, and interdisciplinary research.

## Our Activities
- **Research & Innovation**
- **Academic Programs & Seminars**
- **Student Conferences & Workshops**
- **Science Exhibitions & Outreach**
- **Networking & Collaboration**
- **Science Communication**
- **Competitions & Student Activities**
- **National & International Engagement**

## Join NPSS

Whether you are an undergraduate student, graduate student, researcher, educator, or physics enthusiast, NPSS provides an opportunity to connect with a growing community interested in **physics and scientific advancement in Nepal**.
