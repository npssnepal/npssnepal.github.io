About NPSS

Nepal Physical Students’ Society (NPSS) brings together students of physics and related disciplines to learn, collaborate, and contribute to science through academic and student-led initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a strong, collaborative, and inclusive community of physics students that contributes to the advancement of physics, scientific research, education, and innovation in Nepal.

Our Mission

  • Promote physics education and scientific curiosity.
  • Encourage students to participate in research.
  • Support academic, communication, and leadership skills.
  • Strengthen collaboration across institutions in Nepal.
  • Connect students with national and global communities.
